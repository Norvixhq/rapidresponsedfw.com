GOOGLE ANALYTICS
----------------
GA4 tag G-X22YJKB5YQ is installed on all 58 pages via _source/lib.py.

of lib.py. Change it there and regenerate — never edit the HTML.

Custom events (GA4 does NOT track these by default):
    call_click    fires on any tel: link
    email_click   fires on any mailto: link
Both carry link_location (which button) and page_path (which page).

Give it 24-48 hours to appear in the events list after first traffic.
